Menu item photos (optional)
---------------------------
Item-er nijer photo na thakle site-e BFC logo placeholder dekhay ("Photo coming soon").
Kono item-er photo dite chaile ei folder-e file-ta item-er naam diye rakhun - baki sob automatic.

Naam-er niyom: item-er naam, chhoto hater akshor, space/symbol-er jaygay "-".
Format: jpg, jpeg, png ba webp.

Udaharon:
  Naga Wings                 ->  naga-wings.jpg
  Chicken Pops               ->  chicken-pops.jpg
  Garlic Burger Regular      ->  garlic-burger-regular.webp
  Masala / Garlic Fries      ->  masala-garlic-fries.png
  Mega Feast-1 (for 2 person) -> mega-feast-1-for-2-person.jpg

Ei folder-er photo gulo online photo-r cheye age use hoy.
